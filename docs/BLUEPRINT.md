## Ordering rules

- Domains appear in this order in every module: Craft and Structure → Information and Ideas → Standard English Conventions → Expression of Ideas.
- Within a domain group, skills are grouped and the group starts with its easiest item and ends with its hardest. The second half of each group has a higher mean rating than the first half.
- No two Hard items (rating 4–5) are adjacent anywhere in a module.
- Answer letters are balanced (8/8/7/7) within each module, and no letter appears three times in a row.
- Target times: rating 1→35 s, 2→45 s, 3→60 s, 4→75 s, 5→90 s, plus 10 s for cross-text and quantitative-evidence items. Each module's total is within its 36-minute limit.

## Routing

Module 1 raw score of 18 or more out of 30 (60%) routes to the higher Module 2; 17 or fewer routes to the lower Module 2. This is the routing rule for this practice form only. The real exam routes students with an item-response-theory model and does not publish a raw cutoff.

## Deviations from the real exam

- 60 questions (30 per module) as specified for this form; the real Reading and Writing section has 54 (27 per module, including unscored pretest questions).
- 36 minutes per module instead of 32, keeping the real pace per question.
- Routing uses a stated raw-score threshold instead of an item-response-theory model.
- Scores are reported as raw scores with domain and skill breakdowns; no 200-800 scaled score is estimated.
- Each domain has exactly 15 items (25%); the real exam weights domains slightly differently.
